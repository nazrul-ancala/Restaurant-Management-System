# Restaurant Management System

## Overview

The Restaurant Management System (RMS) is a web-based application designed to streamline the daily operations of small and medium-sized restaurants. It provides a centralized platform for managing restaurant activities, including menu management, table management, customer orders, kitchen workflow, inventory tracking, employee roles, payments, and business reporting.

## Problem Statement

Many restaurants still rely on manual processes or disconnected systems, resulting in:

- Slow order processing
- Communication gaps between waiters and kitchen staff
- Inaccurate inventory records
- Human errors during order taking
- Limited visibility into business performance

## Objectives

- Digitize restaurant operations
- Improve communication between staff
- Reduce ordering errors
- Automate inventory updates
- Provide real-time business reports
- Improve customer service efficiency
- Maintain a complete inventory audit trail.

## Target Users

| Role | Responsibilities |
|------|------------------|
| Administrator | Manage the entire system and user accounts |
| Manager | Monitor operations, reports, inventory, and menu |
| Waiter | Manage tables and customer orders |
| Chef | Process and update food preparation status |
| Cashier | Handle payments and complete orders |

## Project Scope (Version 1)

- Role-Based Access Control
- Employee Management

- Category Management

    - Takeaway Orders

Inventory Management

- Reports

### Excluded

- Online Ordering
- Delivery Management
- Mobile Application
- Third-Party Payment Gateway
- Multi-Branch Support
- Loyalty Program

## Success Criteria

- Waiters can create a customer order within 30 seconds.
- Kitchen receives new orders within 2 seconds.
- Inventory updates automatically after completed orders.
- Managers can view daily sales reports in real time.
- Every important action is recorded in an audit log.
